# Recommendation: LAUNCH for scientific fit, focused on larger t=3 cells

There is now a credible path to visible frontier progress: the sprint produced
**C(48,9,3)<=272 versus published 273, and C(90,12,3)<=678 versus published 679**,
using modest weighted-search continuations. Exact coverage passed separate implementations.
This changes the picture from the initial sweep's historical rediscoveries and
unsuccessful generic searches. However, two author-checked candidates and a small,
adaptively selected sample do not establish a dependable donor success rate.
The proposals remain unaccepted pending separate review and a fresh novelty audit.

The research evidence supports an inaugural launch centered on the larger t=3
track. This recommendation does not authorize publication or open donor intake.
The old small-instance onboarding path should be retired. Six shortlisted current
witness counts still need to be obtained or reproduced before those cells can be
assigned without setup uncertainty; two cells now have better local proposals.
Separate review, a consenting maintainer and the requested pending governance
decisions must precede an actual public opening.

No A result has been accepted or separately reviewed. Both candidates beat the
audited published sources and have exact mathematical validity; their remaining
novelty/review qualification is explicit. Reaching an existing record or generating
a different unclassified cover is not B. Solver implementation, controls and
faster checking are infrastructure, not mathematical discoveries. The old 184-cell,
2,943-restart greedy sweep remains a negative calibration for that old method;
its trials must not be pooled with this selected weighted-search experiment to
manufacture a donor hit-rate estimate.

## Before public opening and the first eight-session evaluation

1. A **different reviewer** checks both proposed witnesses, source credit and fresh
   successor/literature/propagation comparison. Run its `check_row --proposal`
   command and a separately authored containment check. If an earlier <=272
   construction (or <=678 for the second cell) is found, downgrade its novelty classification and retain the
   valid witness and audit.
2. Before assigning a cell, materialize and verify a current-or-better witness,
   freeze dated comparison counts and hashes, and define an append-only work-unit
   registry. Start with reviewed local witnesses and gate the six missing current
   counts until their access/setup issue is resolved.
3. Run **eight 45-minute evaluation sessions, one per shortlisted cell**, with a
   frozen 5-minute orientation, 30-minute search, 5-minute verification and
   5-minute handoff budget. Randomize task/seed assignment using executed
   randomness. Register disjoint overlap-guided deletion clusters and a weighted
   search control; don't spend the entire budget chasing historical counts.
   Save every attempted unit and UNKNOWN, not only successful witnesses.
4. Reassess after all eight. A reasonable editorial continuation criterion is at least one additional
   separately verified A result, or a reviewed B result with a clear route to a
   source improvement, plus useful nonduplicative artifacts from at least six
   sessions and verification under five minutes per claim. This is a proposed
   continuation criterion, not a statistical confidence guarantee. If no additional A/B
   appears and only routine failures accumulate, pause expansion and reconsider
   the donor offering rather than advertising an unmeasured success rate.
