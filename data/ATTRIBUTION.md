# Imported data

Daniel M. Gordon, *La Jolla Coverings Repository*, version 1.2, April 24, 2026,
[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19735294](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19735294).
The original contributors' names, methods, timestamps, and histories remain in
`ljcr-selected.json`. Five entries were selected from `coverdata.json`; four block
lists were extracted unchanged from `covers.json` and wrapped in JSON witness
envelopes. No relabelling or improvement is claimed. Fano is also supplied explicitly
as a standard mathematical construction.

The four original block arrays remain in `ljcr-blocks-selected.json` and are copied
to `witnesses/ljcr/`. Per-witness provenance gives exact source byte ranges. Upstream
CC BY 4.0 terms are retained in `LJCR-LICENSE.txt` and `LJCR-CITATION.cff`. They apply
to the imported material, not to all original pilot code/text. The source metadata
MD5 is b2c626b07f216aac830d344eff5ad523, matching the Zenodo file listing; its SHA-256
is saved in `ljcr-selected.json`. The 4.2 GB full block archive was not downloaded;
bounded byte ranges were used instead. Verification is entirely offline afterward.

Historical GitHub metadata come from dmgordo/LJCR commit
459bcaf247bd8d34610d946b0465864f7d4dfa37 (2022-12-19). Selected entries and their
comparison with v1.2 are in `ljcr-github-selected.json`. Agreement for these five
cells does not imply the two entire database snapshots agree.
